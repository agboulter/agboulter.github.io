Approach to Learning

My aim is to become a well-respected teacher, creating a classroom where students feel safe, comfortable, and able to speak freely. I want to create a classroom with clear instructional practice and a positive classroom culture fostering student learning. Effective classroom management begins with safety, shown through clear, displayed rules and expectations (Rogers, 2015). This aligns with the Positive Behaviour Support (PBS) model, where explicit modelling and clear expectations guide behaviour and encourage positive learning attitudes (NSW Department of Education, 2022). My approach is also based on Bandura’s Social Cognitive Theory, where the teacher models appropriate behaviours and helps students build self-efficacy (De Nobile et al., 2021). Furthermore, I believe misbehaviour has underlying causes that need to be addressed using strategies promoting engagement, understanding, and meeting student needs, reflecting values from the Responsible Thinking Process, wherein students are seen as critical within their own education. My aim is for their ideas to be part of the classroom culture so they feel represented and are encouraged to make respectful, positive choices (De Nobile et al., 2021).

My Philosophy

All students bring unique identities, abilities, opinions, backgrounds, and attitudes to the classroom. I believe the classroom should be a safe environment where positive behaviours are modelled, supporting both academic and social growth. I endeavour to approach teaching with an open mind, modelling the skills and behaviours I wish to develop in my students, while remaining open to new learning opportunities.
